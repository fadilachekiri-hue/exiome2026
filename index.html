About

eXIOME 2026

The eXIOME (eXpert International Oncology MEeting) is dedicated to the applicability of Western oncology guidelines in Low- and Middle-Income Countries (LMICs).

At the heart of this 3rd edition: the Neoadjuvant-to-Adjuvant treatment continuum — bridging evidence and accessibility for locally advanced cancers.

Three days of science, interactive case-based discussions, expert panels, and symposia, bringing together leading oncologists from the Mediterranean region and beyond.

Call for Abstracts

Abstract Submission Guidelines

📅 Deadline: Monday, October 26th, 2026

Submit to: yazid.belkacemi@aphp.fr  —  CC: fadila.chekiri@aphp.fr

📋 Formal Requirements

Format

  • Maximum 350 words
  • Font: Times New Roman 12pt, spacing 1.5
  • File: Word (.doc / .docx)
  • Maximum one page (incl. figures & references)
  • Margins: Top & Bottom 2.5 cm, Left & Right 1.5 cm

Typography

  • Title: 14pt, Bold, centered
  • Authors: 12pt, centered (underline presenting author)
  • Affiliations & email: 11pt, centered
  • Main text: 12pt, justified
  • References: 11pt, square brackets [1]

📐 Abstract Structure

  • 1. Title (max 70 chars)
  • 2. Introduction
  • 3. Objectives
  • 4. Material & Methods
  • 5. Results
  • 6. Conclusion
