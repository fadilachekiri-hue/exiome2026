Abstract Submission

Submit Your Abstract

AROME Junior investigators are invited to submit abstracts for oral or poster presentation at eXIOME 2026.

📄 Formal Requirements

  • Maximum 350 words
  • Font: Times New Roman, 12pt, 1.5 spacing
  • Maximum 1 page (including figures & references)
  • File format: Microsoft Word (.doc / .docx)
  • Title: max 70 characters

📐 Formatting Details

  • Margins: Top/Bottom 2.5 cm · Left/Right 1.5 cm · Gutter 1 cm
  • Title: 14pt, Bold, centered
  • Authors: 12pt, centered, underline presenting author
  • Affiliations & Email: 11pt, centered
  • Main text: 12pt, justified
  • References: 11pt, square brackets [1]

🎤 Abstract Structure

1
Title
2
Introduction
3
Objectives
4
Material & Methods
5
Results
6
Conclusion
