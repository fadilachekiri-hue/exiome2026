Abstract Submission

Submit Your Abstract

AROME Junior investigators are invited to submit abstracts for oral communications at eXIOME 2026.

Format & Requirements

  • Word document (.doc/.docx)
  • Maximum 350 words
  • Times New Roman, 12pt, 1.5 spacing
  • Title (max 70 characters)
  • Authors + affiliations
  • Structured: Background, Methods, Results, Conclusions
  • No images, tables or references

Scientific Themes

  • Locally advanced cancers in LMICs
  • Neoadjuvant-to-adjuvant treatment continuum
  • Artificial intelligence in oncology
  • Clinical cases with therapeutic innovation

Selection Criteria

  • Scientific quality & originality
  • Clinical relevance for LMICs
  • Clarity of presentation
26 October 2026
